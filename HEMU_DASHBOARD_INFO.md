## Features Available

### 1. Food Orders Tab 🍽️
- View all food orders in real-time
- See customer name, phone, room number/delivery address
- View ordered items with quantities and prices
- Manage order status:
  - **Pending** → Click "Start Preparing"
  - **Preparing** → Click "Mark Ready"
  - **Ready** → Click "Mark Delivered"
  - Can also "Cancel" orders at any stage

### 2. Trek Bookings Tab ⛰️
- View all trek bookings
- See trek name, customer details, date, number of people, and cost
- Manage booking status:
  - **Pending** → Click "Confirm Trek"
  - **Confirmed** → Click "Mark Completed" (after trek is done)
  - Can also "Cancel" bookings
- **Note:** Cannot edit trek details, only manage bookings

### 3. Room Bookings Tab 🏠
- View all room inquiries
- See guest details: name, phone, email
- Check-in and check-out dates
- Number of guests and room type preferences
- Special messages from guests
